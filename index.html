Our Story

A soda fountain for
Long Hill's main street

The Stirling Fountain opened its window in the summer of 2023 and became the town's gathering spot almost overnight — the line of kids on bikes gave it away.

Everything about the place is a little bit yesterday: coupe glasses, egg creams, wafer cookies in every sundae and whipped cream made in-house each morning. Grab a seat in the sunny outdoor spot and stay a while.

  • ✓ Gluten-free friendly, with clearly marked picks
  • ✓ Vegan & no-sugar-added flavors always in the case
  • ✓ Sunny outdoor seating steps from the Passaic trail
